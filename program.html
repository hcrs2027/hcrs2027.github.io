Program

The 3rd Workshop on Human-Centered Recommender Systems (HCRS@WWW 2027) will be held as a full-day session on Tuesday, TBA (during May 3–7, 2027), 10:00–13:30 (IST, UTC+1), held online ().

The program features two keynote talks, an oral session with six talks, and the Best Paper Award announcement. All times are in Irish Standard Time (IST, UTC+1).

Program Schedule

11:10–12:22 Oral Presentations (6 talks)
12:22–12:40 Best Paper Award & Closing Remarks
