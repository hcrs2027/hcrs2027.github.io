Program

The 3rd Workshop on Human-Centered Recommender Systems (HCRS@WWW 2027) is proposed as a full-day, in-person workshop during The Web Conference 2027, held May 3–7, 2027 in Dublin, Ireland. The specific workshop day and session times will be announced once the conference program is finalized.

The planned program features four 30-minute keynote talks, three paper sessions (oral presentations), a poster session, and a panel discussion. All times below are shown in Irish Standard Time (IST, UTC+1) and are tentative.

Notes

  • The exact workshop date (one of May 3–7, 2027) and the final session times will be published here once the conference program is finalized.
  • Accepted papers will be split between oral presentations in the three paper sessions and posters in the poster session. The oral / poster assignment and the per-talk duration will be announced together with the accepted paper list.
  • Keynote speaker names, talk titles, and abstracts will appear on the Keynote page as invitations are confirmed.
  • The workshop is an in-person event, consistent with The Web Conference 2027 policy that workshops take place in person.
