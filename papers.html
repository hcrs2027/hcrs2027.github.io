  • RecoWorld: Building Simulated Environments for Agentic Recommender Systems🏆 Best Paper
    Fei Liu, Xinyu Lin, Hanchao Yu, Mingyuan Wu, Jianyu Wang, Qiang Zhang, Zhuokai Zhao, Yinglong Xia, Yao Zhang, Weiwei Li, Mingze Gao, Qifan Wang, Lizhu Zhang, Benyu Zhang, Xiangjun Fan
    OpenReview PDF
    Award Citation. This paper presents RecoWorld, a simulated environment framework that enables multi-turn interactions between simulated users and agentic recommender systems. The core contribution lies in its dual-view architecture where users provide natural language instructions and the recommender learns to follow them iteratively, shifting the evaluation paradigm from static offline metrics toward dynamic, instruction-driven feedback. This design philosophy resonates strongly with the spirit of HCRS, as it treats user agency and long-term satisfaction as first-class objectives rather than byproducts of engagement optimization. By formulating the problem in a Gym-like RL framework with retention-based reward signals, the work lays important groundwork for the community to build recommender systems that are genuinely responsive to human intent.
