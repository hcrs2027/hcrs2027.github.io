Accepted Papers

The 3rd Workshop on Human-Centered Recommender Systems (HCRS@WWW 2027) has been proposed as a full-day, in-person workshop during The Web Conference 2027 (May 10–14, 2027, Dublin, Ireland). Workshop proposal notification is expected in October 2026.

If the proposal is accepted, the call for papers will open in late 2026 and the list of accepted papers will be published on this page following paper notification.
